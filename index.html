01 / OUR STORY

WHY CITYMOMVIBESSA

A journey that became a space for other women.

CITYMOMVIBESSA was born from a personal journey through women's health challenges, motherhood, grief, pelvic floor dysfunction, infertility, pregnancy, postpartum highs and lows, low libido, weight changes and the transition into perimenopause and menopause.

After finding support from practitioners and counsellors, the idea grew: to share the journey and help women understand that they do not have to endure in silence.

“Each time a woman stands up for herself, without knowing it possibly, without claiming it, she stands up for all women.”— Maya Angelou
